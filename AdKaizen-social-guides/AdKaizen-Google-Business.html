5. Tips for Google Business Profile

  • Google will ask you to verify the business (video, phone or postcard). Do this first: updates don't show until it's verified.
  • Set each update's button to Book with the booking link, or Learn more with the website.
  • Add 5–10 photos in the Photos tab: banners and carousel covers are fine to start.
  • Reviews matter most here. Ask every happy client for a Google review and reply to each one within a day.
  • Turn on chat/messages if Google offers it, and answer within a few hours.
