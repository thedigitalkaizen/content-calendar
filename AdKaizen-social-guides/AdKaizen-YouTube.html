5. Tips for YouTube Shorts

  • Film vertical (1080×1920), 20–40 seconds. Say the hook in the first 2 seconds and show it as on-screen text too.
  • Each script below has the title to use. Keep #shorts at the end of the title.
  • Reuse every Short as an Instagram Reel the same day (download it without a watermark from your editor, not from YouTube).
  • Pin a comment with the free strategy call link under each Short.
