5. Tips for Instagram

  • Before sharing the profile anywhere, post the first 6 carousels so the grid isn't empty. You can post 2 a day for 3 days, then switch to the calendar.
  • Pin 3 posts: 01 One test a week, 08 Free strategy call, 02 Cheap leads.
  • Highlights need a story first: post a slide as a story, then add it to a highlight and set the cover.
  • Stories most days: share each new post to your story, add a poll or question sticker ("What's your cost per lead?").
  • Hashtags are included in each caption. Spend 10 minutes after posting replying to comments and to accounts in your niche.
  • Connect @adkaizen to the Facebook Page in Meta Business Suite. You can then schedule both from one place.

6. After Mon 21 Dec

After the 10 carousels, aim for 2 carousels + 1 Reel a week. Turn your best carousel into a Reel (use the YouTube Shorts scripts), and reuse slides as stories.
